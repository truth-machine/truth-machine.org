## What Truth-Machine is (and is not)

- Not a language model  
- Not a decision engine  
- Not a rules checklist  

Truth-Machine is a governance layer for *interpretive influence*.

---

## LLMs, NLP, and Truth-Machine {#llms-nlp-and-truth-machine}

- **LLMs** generate language.
- **NLP** analyzes and labels language.
- **Truth-Machine** governs how language is allowed to influence decisions.

> Truth-Machine does not replace human judgment. It makes reasoning explicit, traceable, and defensible.

---

## Does this compete with existing AI tools?

No. Truth-Machine complements them by providing governed evaluations and traceable rationale that external tools can interrogate without inventing post-hoc explanations.
